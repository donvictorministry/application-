About Us & Our App

Multi-Utility Pro

The App is designed with a singular vision: to empower users with reliable, focused tools for daily productivity.

Whether you want to read the entire New Testament in sixty days, calculate financial stewardship, schedule prayer alerts, or organize your spiritual calendar, this platform exists to support your mission without the distractions of modern bloatware.

How to Use

  • New Testament Reading Plan

    A complete digital reading guide with daily streaks and visual progress. Each day you complete your reading, your streak increases, and your progress percentage updates, giving you a clear picture of your journey. Whether you believe you need this now or later, the truth remains: no one can accomplish anything of lasting value without God. Do not wait until later. Start today.

  • Loan Calculator: Input your principal, term, and rate to forecast financial stewardship.
  • Smart Calc: Toggle the top-left button for advanced scientific operations.
  • Calendar: Tap arrows to navigate. Days marked with red dots indicate global prayer events.
  • Alarms: Set daily times to trigger continuous, screen-locking reminders for prayer.
